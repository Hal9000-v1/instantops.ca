About Instant Ops

Practical expertise for faster, higher-quality results

Instant Ops was established in 2008 based on observations that hold true for most organizations:

  • There is a strong desire for significantly better efficiency and effectiveness.
  • These improvements require optimized processes and policies.
  • Internal experts are generally too busy with core responsibilities to invest the time required to establish, document, and institutionalize process and policy improvements.
  • Most consultants lack the experience, expertise, and focus needed to create practical solutions for unique environments within reasonable time and cost levels.
  • The use of templated materials is acceptable and desirable as long as it provides pragmatic value and can be tailored to specific requirements.

Since its inception, Instant Ops has provided a wide range of services to a wide variety of customers. Engagements have ranged from improving sales positioning to deploying new IT Operations methodologies. Customers have ranged from start-ups to large corporations.

The common theme across engagements has been the rapid pace at which the work was conducted and concluded, the high quality of the service, successful outcomes, and a high level of customer satisfaction.

Rapid service delivery is further accelerated when customers include one or more of the pre-built documents and/or leverage structured service. Service options are broad; the examples on this site are representative of the full range of possibilities.
