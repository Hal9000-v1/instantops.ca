Documents and structured services that accelerate improvement

Instant Ops products include documents and structured services. Documents can enhance a broader engagement or be purchased stand-alone. Structured service follows a scripted process with pre-defined methodology and output, providing a clear value proposition and understanding of ROI.

Documentation Products

Pre-built IT Operations best-practice documents that contain the essentials required to define and describe key IT Operations processes. They help organizations cut months of development time and move more rapidly and accurately toward finalized process definition and documentation.

These products provide an excellent link between service strategy and service delivery. They include process flow charts (function-by-role-by-context detail) and policy documents that supply pragmatic process and outcome-based guidance for core IT operations functions.

Inbound Marketing Assessment

A structured service designed to help organizations understand the nature and potential of Inbound Marketing. It follows a prescriptive process that educates on IM techniques and platforms, then determines an optimal approach based on corporate strengths and goals.

Activities are grouped into five categories:

  • IM Orientation
  • Audit of current assets related to IM
  • Alignment with corporate goals
  • Development of specific and measurable IM objectives
  • Plan creation that maps a path to meeting those objectives within target time frames
